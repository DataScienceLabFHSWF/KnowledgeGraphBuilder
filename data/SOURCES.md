## Citation & Attribution

When using this corpus or derived datasets, please cite the source authorities and documents accordingly. Each document is authored by the respective German federal state or regulatory body (Bavarian, Lower Saxonian, Schleswig-Holsteinian, Baden-Württemberg, and Hessian environmental/nuclear safety ministries).

**Accessibility Note**: These documents were publicly available as of June 28, 2024. Current accessibility may vary. Archived versions are available upon request.
